# Johto postgame plan (owner decisions 2026-11-02; nothing built yet)

Sequence (owner):
1. First Hall of Fame (Kanto league) -> credits -> the player wakes in their room in Pallet Town (Oak's speech in the Hall of Fame stays).
2. Oak gives a ticket for the S.S. Aqua (Vermilion -> Olivine). The Pokegear: if the player does not have it, their mother stops them at the door and gives it before they leave the house.
3. NO other way into Johto until the ferry trip is done (Route 22 gate's Johto arm, Magnet Train, Fly, Victory Road, other boats stay closed). The Town Map shows Kanto ONLY and the Pokedex stays Kanto-only until the Pokedex upgrade (below).
4. First arrival in Johto: Elm phones and asks the player to come to New Bark Town. From then on Fly to New Bark Town is allowed (after the call).
5. Elm's lab: a new starter (Chikorita, Cyndaquil or Totodile), then the Mega Ring; Elm has no Mega Stones and sends the player to Mr. Pokemon; Mr. Pokemon gives the Mega Stone matching the Johto starter and Mega Evolution unlocks. At Mr. Pokemon's house Oak upgrades the Pokedex (Johto + National); until then the Pokedex is Kanto-only. (Replaces the earlier "Elm upgrades it on the ferry" idea.)
6. The rest of Johto plays as in HeartGold with dialog changes, but wild levels, trainer levels, trainer team sizes and Gym Leader teams all scale with the number of Johto badges owned (curve and numbers still to be given by the owner).
7. The JOHTO LEAGUE needs all 16 badges: the Johto Elite Four (Will, Koga, Bruno, Karen) plus Lance, each with SIX Pokemon, all level 100 (owner 2026-11-02: there is no 8-badge Johto league). Red on Mt. Silver also needs 16 badges (vanilla). Kanto gym rematches stay as long as they exist in the ROM. Safari Zone = later polish.
8. Hall of Fame: win #1 = the Kanto league (Oak's speech); win #2 = the Johto league (Lance's HeartGold speech); wins #3 and later get NO speech.
9. Yellow's S.S. Anne never returns after the player leaves with Cut, so the Vermilion dock is free for the Aqua.

Testing items that STAY until the owner finishes testing (owner 2026-11-02): the Mega flag in the player's bedroom, the Vermilion shared Mart with free Mega Stones / scaling stock. The Vermilion stock expansion is to be extended to ALL Kanto Marts.

Custom Mega: Typhlosion has no Mega in the game's data (Meganium and Feraligatr do); the owner wants a custom Mega Typhlosion if possible. It needs one more species row (rows 1554-1559 and 1583-1585 are used, 1560 is the Stellareon follower slot): another table growth like megas_part2.py, plus art.

Build order (agreed 2026-11-02, one checkpoint ROM each): (1) credits, Pallet room, Mom's Pokegear (a plain flag check: only if the player lacks it), Oak's ticket, the ferry, every other way into Johto closed; (2) Elm's call, Fly to New Bark Town, Town Map and Pokedex locks, Oak's upgrade; (3) Elm's lab, starter, Mega Ring, Mr. Pokemon's stone, Mega unlock, Mart expansion to all Kanto Marts; (4) the 16-badge Johto league (L100 six-Pokemon teams), Lance's speech for win #2, Red; (5) level/team scaling of Johto (last: biggest). Obedience: the Viridian Gym badge (the last Kanto badge) must still make ALL Pokemon obey.

Open questions / checks: Typhlosion Mega art and stats; how the Pokedex and Town Map can be limited to Kanto (a flag check or a data change); whether obedience counts the Kanto badges for Johto play; scaling curve numbers; flags to allocate (below 0xAA0, grep every bank first).

Owner 2026-11-02 (final): the Hall of Fame speech of win #1 is spoken BY Oak (as now). All five build steps approved. Red's team on Mt. Silver is ALSO all level 100.
